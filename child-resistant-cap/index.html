Child Resistant Cap

Published 2026-11-14 · KIWL Plastic

Where a child resistant cap specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the plastic packaging buyers requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a child resistant cap performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 250–500 ml product and delivers 1280–1930 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

Rated capacity of 1280–1930 units per hour is only meaningful alongside the 316L stainless steel contact-part grade and the control system, because the cheaper version of each trades directly against accuracy.

Integration With Your Line

The best commissioning plan names three people: one for the 1.5 ml verification, one for the encoder handshake, one for the cleaning route. With a 1280–1930 units per hour machine, that is what a week-long delay costs.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Look at the child resistant cap over five years rather than one budget cycle. 316L stainless steel contact parts, a 1.5 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover have different cost curves, and the purchase price tells you very little about any of them.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this child resistant cap the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 8-minute routine service realistic on a 1280 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the child resistant cap need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1900 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

How long does a format changeover take on this child resistant cap?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 250–500 ml format takes longer.

How is the child resistant cap documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
