Cleaning Spray Bottle

Published 2026-11-18 · KIWL Plastic

A cleaning spray bottle sits on the critical path of the plastic packaging buyers production line, which makes the selection decision more consequential than the specification sheet suggests. What follows is the working detail — capacity, materials, control architecture and the acceptance checks — for teams evaluating a cleaning spray bottle.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a cleaning spray bottle is not the demonstration but the third month. At 2163–2913 units per hour for 8 oz product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A cleaning spray bottle with a 15-minute changeover across 8 oz format and 304/316L stainless steel contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 2163–2913 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

Confirm the working width of 1200 mm against the actual conveyor pitch, not against a catalogue. That single check removes most of the surprises that appear after a cleaning spray bottle arrives on a plastic packaging buyers floor.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the cleaning spray bottle to real 8 oz volume, then let the 0.3 ml tolerance and the 2163–2913 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

For plastic packaging buyers operations the practical measure of after-sales support is how quickly a cleaning spray bottle returns to 2163 units per hour after a fault — which depends on part availability, not on the number of people in the service team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Can the cleaning spray bottle handle a 8 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 15-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How does the cleaning spray bottle behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the cleaning spray bottle determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
