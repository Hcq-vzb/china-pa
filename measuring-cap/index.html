What This Equipment Does

Consider what a measuring cap removes from the plastic packaging buyers process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 60–120 mm diameter product at 9751–13251 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a measuring cap specification for what is measurable: rate 9751–13251 units per hour, deviation ±0.3 ml, container 60–120 mm diameter, control 10-inch colour touch, load 26–37 kW, footprint 1200 × 1900 × 3200 mm, changeover 12 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the measuring cap determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 9751–13251 units per hour and a 12-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a measuring cap is arithmetic: every unit of 0.3 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 12-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the plastic packaging buyers production line.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive work on the measuring cap is concentrated on the wear parts, and the recommended interval is derived from the 9751–13251 duty range rather than from a generic calendar figure.
