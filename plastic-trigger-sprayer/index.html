Plastic Trigger Sprayer

Published 2026-10-21 · KIWL Plastic

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a plastic trigger sprayer for plastic packaging buyers. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

A plastic trigger sprayer should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 60–120 mm diameter product at 6703–9703 units per hour, which is the span most plastic packaging buyers production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 6703–9703 units per hour means little without 60–120 mm diameter product, a 1.5 ml tolerance and a 20-minute changeover — all three stated for this plastic trigger sprayer.

Integration With Your Line

A plastic trigger sprayer fits an existing plastic packaging buyers production line when three things align: the transfer pitch, the control protocol and the cleaning route. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and works across a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the plastic trigger sprayer on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and a 1.5 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same plastic packaging buyers production line.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this plastic trigger sprayer are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the plastic trigger sprayer?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 60–120 mm diameter set is included.

What is the actual connected load of the plastic trigger sprayer?

The reference build draws 1.5–2.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

What spare parts should be held for the plastic trigger sprayer?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 6703 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
