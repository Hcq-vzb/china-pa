43mm Neck PET Preform

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Plastic

There is no universal 43mm neck pet preform for plastic packaging buyers. The correct configuration depends on the product, the format and the shift pattern. This guide walks through the decision in the order a buyer makes it: what the machine must do, what it must be made of, what it costs to run.

What This Equipment Does

A 43mm neck pet preform earns a place on the plastic packaging buyers production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 17660–22160 units per hour on 330 ml product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

For a 43mm neck pet preform aimed at plastic packaging buyers operations, the practical reference build is 304/316L stainless steel contact parts, a 4-inch HMI and a 1.5–2.5 kW connected load inside 1800 × 2800 × 2400 mm.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the 43mm neck pet preform determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 17660–22160 units per hour and a 15-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the 43mm neck pet preform on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 330 ml product with a 15-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Availability on a 43mm neck pet preform is decided by how quickly a worn part is replaced. Keep the seals, drive and guide rails in stock, because on a 17660–22160 units per hour machine an hour of downtime is a costly hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the delivery lead time for the 43mm neck pet preform?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 330 ml set is included.

What is the actual connected load of the 43mm neck pet preform?

The reference build draws 1.5–2.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

What spare parts should be held for the 43mm neck pet preform?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 17660 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
