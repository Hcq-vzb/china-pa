What This Equipment Does

A PET cosmetic bottle occupies one defined station, and the value of that station is decided by how little attention it needs. This plastic packaging buyers build runs at 3397–4897 units per hour on 500 ml product and is specified so a shift operator can run it without specialist knowledge.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that limits output on a PET cosmetic bottle is usually changeover. At 10 minutes per 500 ml format and 3397–4897 units per hour, campaigns have to be planned around the stop.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the plastic packaging buyers team will adopt the routine — and a PET cosmetic bottle with a 10-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the PET cosmetic bottle on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 500 ml product with a 10-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

The parts worth stocking for a PET cosmetic bottle are small, common and cheap — seals, guide rails, sensor sets. Holding them turns a plastic packaging buyers shutdown into a shift adjustment instead of a week of waiting.

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 3397 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.
