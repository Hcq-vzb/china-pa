Cap Liner Supplier

Published 2026-10-27 · KIWL Plastic

Choosing the right cap liner supplier is a practical decision for plastic packaging buyers. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting a cap liner supplier on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

The cap liner supplier is where raw material becomes a finished unit, and that conversion is where a plastic packaging buyers operation's cost is set. At 15120–19120 units per hour for 500 ml product, this build makes that conversion repeatable.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a cap liner supplier on rated capacity alone. A 15120–19120 units per hour machine with a 1.0 ml tolerance and SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

Integration usually fails at the handshake, not the mechanics. This cap liner supplier takes a standard encoder signal and a 3200 mm working width, so it can run under the control of the line PLC rather than as an isolated island.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the cap liner supplier against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 15120–19120 units per hour a typical plastic packaging buyers operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

For a plastic packaging buyers operation the maintenance question for a cap liner supplier is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the cap liner supplier?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 15120 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Can the cap liner supplier be upgraded later?

Within the 15120–19120 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 500 ml range before ordering.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 4-inch HMI stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for plastic packaging buyers operations running audits.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
