Plastic Dropper Bottle

Published 2026-11-09 · KIWL Plastic

Specifying a plastic dropper bottle for plastic packaging buyers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

The plastic dropper bottle is where raw material becomes a finished unit, and that conversion is where a plastic packaging buyers operation's cost is set. At 5597–7097 units per hour for 8 oz product, this build makes that conversion repeatable.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 5597–7097 units per hour means little without 8 oz product, a 0.5 ml tolerance and a 20-minute changeover — all three stated for this plastic dropper bottle.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This plastic dropper bottle already handles a standard encoder interface and a 1600 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a plastic dropper bottle is downtime avoided during a 20-minute format change on a 8 oz format, because the plastic packaging buyers production line loses output that cannot be recovered later in the day.

Maintenance and Service

Build the maintenance plan from the duty cycle rather than the calendar. At 5597–7097 units per hour the plastic dropper bottle accumulates hours faster than any inspection schedule assumes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the plastic dropper bottle?

The reference build draws 7.5–10.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

What accuracy can this plastic dropper bottle hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.5 ml on 8 oz product at 5597–7097 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.
