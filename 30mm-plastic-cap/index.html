What This Equipment Does

A 30mm plastic cap occupies one defined station, and the value of that station is decided by how little attention it needs. This plastic packaging buyers build runs at 3489–4989 units per hour on 20 L product and is specified so a shift operator can run it without specialist knowledge.

Key Technical Specifications

For a 30mm plastic cap aimed at plastic packaging buyers operations, the practical reference build is 304/316L stainless steel contact parts, a 10-inch colour touch and a 7.5–10.5 kW connected load inside 1600 × 2800 × 3200 mm.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the 30mm plastic cap determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 3489–4989 units per hour and a 10-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the 30mm plastic cap to real 20 L volume, then let the 0.5 ml tolerance and the 3489–4989 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Availability planning for a 30mm plastic cap starts with the assumption that a seal will need replacing in the next quarter. Holding the 304/316L stainless steel contact-part spares on site is what keeps a 10-minute repair a 10-minute repair.
