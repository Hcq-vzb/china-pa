Cosmetic Airless Jar

Published 2026-11-17 · KIWL Plastic

Where a cosmetic airless jar specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the plastic packaging buyers requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

The unit a cosmetic airless jar performs is easy to describe and hard to do well: it takes 5 L product and delivers 10386–13886 units per hour, and everything downstream is calibrated against that figure.

Key Technical Specifications

A cosmetic airless jar is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 10386–13886 units per hour, contact parts 304/316L stainless steel, control 4-inch HMI, load 6.5–8.5 kW, footprint 2400 × 1400 × 3200 mm, changeover 8 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This cosmetic airless jar already handles a standard encoder interface and a 2400 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A cosmetic airless jar at 10386–13886 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 5 L product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the plastic packaging buyers operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive work on the cosmetic airless jar is concentrated on the wear parts, and the recommended interval is derived from the 10386–13886 duty range rather than from a generic calendar figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is support handled after commissioning?

What is the actual connected load of the cosmetic airless jar?

The reference build draws 6.5–8.5 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.
