The airless pump bottle is easier to specify than most plastic packaging buyers buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

For a plastic packaging buyers operation, the airless pump bottle is where a production target either becomes repeatable or stays theoretical. This unit runs at 4374–5874 units per hour on 1 L product with a measured deviation rather than a quoted one.

Key Technical Specifications

Price an airless pump bottle on the specification that limits your output: the 0.5 ml tolerance, the SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and the 8-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This airless pump bottle already handles a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. An airless pump bottle with a 10-inch colour touch and a 8-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a plastic packaging buyers buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

The airless pump bottle keeps its wear items accessible and its parameters exportable, so a shift leader can restore a setting without calling a technician and without losing 4374 units per hour of output.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the airless pump bottle do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How does the airless pump bottle behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the airless pump bottle determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

Is a SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 1 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
