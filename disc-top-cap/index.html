Disc Top Cap

Published 2026-11-04 · KIWL Plastic

Choosing the right disc top cap is a practical decision for plastic packaging buyers. This guide covers the criteria that matter when specifying, integrating and accepting a disc top cap on a production line, with the figures a buyer should confirm in writing before placing an order.

What This Equipment Does

The test of a disc top cap is not the demonstration but the third month. At 11274–14774 units per hour for 500 ml can product, this configuration is designed to hold its rate once the novelty of commissioning has worn off.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask what a supplier is not willing to write down. The answer, on a disc top cap, is usually the tolerance at the low end of the range: this build holds 0.5 ml at 11274 units per hour, not only at 14774.

Integration With Your Line

A disc top cap that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 2400 mm working width, which is what lets the plastic packaging buyers production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service arrangement in the numbers. A disc top cap with a 7-inch colour touch and a 15-minute changeover has a running cost that can be predicted, and predictability is worth more to a plastic packaging buyers buyer than a lower price.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the disc top cap service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the disc top cap run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 7-inch colour touch, yes. The plastic packaging buyers team still owns the routine checks, but the 15-minute changeover is guided.

What spare parts should be held for the disc top cap?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 11274 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

How is the disc top cap documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
