Plastic Sprayer Pump

Published 2026-10-26 · KIWL Plastic

The plastic sprayer pump is easier to specify than most plastic packaging buyers buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

A plastic sprayer pump earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 4226–5726 units per hour for 16 oz product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

A plastic sprayer pump is easy to compare when four numbers are isolated from the option list. Here they are: ±0.5 ml, 4226–5726 units per hour, 17–24 kW, and 2400 × 1900 × 2800 mm, on 16 oz product.

Integration With Your Line

Before ordering a plastic sprayer pump, write the interface list and assign an owner to each item: encoder, safety relay, product detection, cleaning return. This configuration already handles a standard encoder interface and a 2400 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The business case for a plastic sprayer pump is arithmetic before it is technical: 0.5 ml per unit, 4226–5726 units per hour, and a shift count that adds up across a year. Work it that way and the answer is usually within 12–36 months at regional plastic packaging buyers scale.

Maintenance and Service

Ask the supplier to run a maintenance walkthrough with the shift team before the plastic sprayer pump leaves the factory. On this build the routine points — seals, drive, guide rails — are reachable with ordinary tools.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the plastic sprayer pump do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

Can the plastic sprayer pump run without a dedicated operator?

Once the recipes are stored on the 10-inch colour touch, yes. The plastic packaging buyers team still owns the routine checks, but the 8-minute changeover is guided.

What accuracy can this plastic sprayer pump hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.5 ml on 16 oz product at 4226–5726 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
