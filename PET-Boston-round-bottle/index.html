PET Boston Round Bottle

A PET Boston round bottle for plastic packaging buyers is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

Consider what a PET Boston round bottle removes from the plastic packaging buyers process: a step, a wait, or a dependency on operator skill. This configuration handles 500 ml can product at 18104–22604 units per hour and is built around that single job.

Key Technical Specifications

For a plastic packaging buyers buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: food-grade 304 stainless steel product-contact parts, 7-inch colour touch controls, 0.3 ml tolerance, 12-minute changeover, 6.5–8.5 kW connected load and a 1200 × 1900 × 2100 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

A PET Boston round bottle that shares its control protocol with the rest of the plastic packaging buyers production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the PET Boston round bottle on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 500 ml can product with a 12-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive discipline on a PET Boston round bottle is easier when the intervals are stated in cycles rather than months. At 18104–22604 units per hour, this build's seals and sensors reach end of life far sooner than a quarterly check would suggest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the PET Boston round bottle need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1200 × 1900 × 2100 mm GA drawing.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

How does the PET Boston round bottle behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the PET Boston round bottle determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
