Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the petg cosmetic bottle?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 15253 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Is a food-grade 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 250–500 ml product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What is the actual connected load of the petg cosmetic bottle?

The reference build draws 39–54 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
