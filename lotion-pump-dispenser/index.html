Lotion Pump Dispenser

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Plastic

The lotion pump dispenser is easier to specify than most plastic packaging buyers buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

A lotion pump dispenser earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 1208–1858 units per hour for 12 oz product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 1208–1858 units per hour means little without 12 oz product, a 1.0 ml tolerance and a 8-minute changeover — all three stated for this lotion pump dispenser.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the plastic packaging buyers team will adopt the routine — and a lotion pump dispenser with a 8-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest case for a lotion pump dispenser is the downtime it prevents during a 8-minute changeover. At 1208 units per hour, the plastic packaging buyers production line recovers exactly what the stop takes away.

Maintenance and Service

The lotion pump dispenser was designed so the routine work happens during a changeover rather than during production. Seals, sensors and drives are grouped for access, which is what allows a 1208 unit per hour plastic packaging buyers production line to maintain without a shutdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the lotion pump dispenser handle a 12 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 8-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

Can the lotion pump dispenser be upgraded later?

Within the 1208–1858 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 12 oz range before ordering.

How long does a format changeover take on this lotion pump dispenser?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 8 minutes. The first change to a new 12 oz format takes longer.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
