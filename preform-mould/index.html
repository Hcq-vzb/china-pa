Frequently Asked Questions

How is the preform mould documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

What accuracy can this preform mould hold in real production?

The reference build holds 0.5 ml on 20 L product at 2164–3164 units per hour. Confirm the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.
