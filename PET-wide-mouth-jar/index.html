PET Wide Mouth Jar

Published 2026-11-21 · KIWL Plastic

Specifying a PET wide mouth jar for plastic packaging buyers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

What a PET wide mouth jar does to the plastic packaging buyers production line is measurable at the packing end. At 6442–9442 units per hour for 500 ml can product, the shift output stops depending on how the previous hour went.

Key Technical Specifications

Price a PET wide mouth jar on the specification that limits your output: the 0.5 ml tolerance, the 316L stainless steel contact parts and the 12-minute format changeover. Those three determine both the running cost and how often the line has to stop.

Integration With Your Line

Downstream transfer matters as much as upstream buffer. If the PET wide mouth jar can hand over at 6442–9442 units per hour without accumulation, the rest of the line keeps its own cadence.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Three mechanisms produce the return on a PET wide mouth jar: less material lost, fewer hours worked, less time stopped. At a 0.5 ml tolerance and 6442–9442 units per hour, all three move together for a plastic packaging buyers operation.

Maintenance and Service

The parts worth stocking for a PET wide mouth jar are small, common and cheap — seals, guide rails, sensor sets. Holding them turns a plastic packaging buyers shutdown into a shift adjustment instead of a week of waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the PET wide mouth jar?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 6442 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

What does the PET wide mouth jar do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

What is the delivery lead time for the PET wide mouth jar?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 500 ml can set is included.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
