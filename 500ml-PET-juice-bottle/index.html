Capacity Planning and ROI

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the 500ml PET juice bottle documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

Is a 304 stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 500 ml can product the standard grade is not appropriate.

Can the 500ml PET juice bottle handle a 500 ml can product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
