Finger Sprayer 18-415

Published 2026-10-28 · KIWL Plastic

Specifying a finger sprayer 18-415 for plastic packaging buyers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

For a plastic packaging buyers operation, the finger sprayer 18-415 is where a production target either becomes repeatable or stays theoretical. This unit runs at 10655–14155 units per hour on 5 L product with a measured deviation rather than a quoted one.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a finger sprayer 18-415 specification for what is measurable: rate 10655–14155 units per hour, deviation ±1.0 ml, container 5 L, control 4-inch HMI, load 3–4 kW, footprint 1200 × 1900 × 3200 mm, changeover 10 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the plastic packaging buyers team will adopt the routine — and a finger sprayer 18-415 with a 10-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a finger sprayer 18-415 is arithmetic: every unit of 1.0 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 10-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the plastic packaging buyers production line.

Maintenance and Service

Remote support matters for a finger sprayer 18-415 because the fastest diagnoses come from the actual machine data: a 4-inch HMI that records the last 10 minutes of a fault tells you more than a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this finger sprayer 18-415?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 5 L format takes longer.

Can the finger sprayer 18-415 handle a 5 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 10-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 4-inch HMI.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
